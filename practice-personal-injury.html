Know your deadline

California limits how long you have to file.

For most personal injury claims the statute of limitations is two years from the date of injury. Against a government entity it can be as little as six months. Missing it can end your case entirely.

Common questions

Personal injury FAQ

How much does it cost to hire you? +
Nothing upfront. Personal injury cases are handled on a contingency fee, meaning we are paid a percentage of what we recover and owe nothing if there is no recovery.
How long will my case take? +
Most injury claims resolve in six to eighteen months. Cases that go to trial can take longer. We give you a realistic timeline at the first meeting.
What should I do right after an accident? +
Get medical attention, document everything, avoid giving recorded statements to insurers, and contact us before signing anything. Our free guide below walks you through it.
