Business & Corporate FAQ

Do I need a lawyer to form my LLC? +
You can form one yourself, but the operating agreement and tax structure decisions you make in the first month often matter for years. We handle it right from the start.
Can you act as our general counsel? +
Yes. Many clients use us as fractional outside general counsel, paying for what they need rather than a full time in house salary.
