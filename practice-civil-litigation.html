Common questions

Civil Litigation FAQ

How much does litigation cost? +
Every case is different. We scope matters in phases with clear estimates so you stay in control of the budget.
Do I have to go to trial? +
Most disputes settle before trial. That said, we prepare every matter as though it will be tried, which improves outcomes whether or not it goes to a jury.
How long does a lawsuit take? +
From a few months for a negotiated resolution to two years or more for complex matters that reach trial.
